# BuildCrew — Strategic Product Roadmap & Future Implementations

> **Platform:** BuildCrew (Campus Collab & Collegiate Innovation Circuit)  
> **Prepared For:** Founder & Core Team  
> **Target Release:** Web & Google Play Store  
> **Date:** October 2026

---

## Executive Summary

BuildCrew is positioned as Karnataka’s premier collegiate engineering collaboration engine. With the automated multi-platform ingestion engine delivering 130+ deduplicated, active hackathons across Unstop, Devfolio, HackerEarth, MLH, and national circuits, the core platform foundation is solid and verified.

This roadmap outlines **seven high-impact, unique features** designed to drive organic student adoption, viral campus growth, and superior utility ahead of the mobile Play Store launch.

---

## 1. AI Squad Role-Gap & Compatibility Engine 🧩

### Problem Statement
The primary reason student hackathon squads fail or drop out mid-event is **unbalanced skill sets** (e.g., three frontend developers with no backend API engineer, or four coders with nobody capable of delivering the business pitch).

### Proposed Solution
An intelligent squad composition analyzer that runs when a team is formed:
- **Squad Balance Metric:** Real-time readiness score (e.g., `85% Ready`).
- **Gap Detection:** Compares the target hackathon's specific tracks (e.g., FinTech, Web3, AI/ML) against the squad members' verified profiles.
- **Actionable Notification:** *"⚠️ Your squad has 2 Frontend builders and 1 AI engineer, but is missing a Backend / Cloud deployment specialist and a Pitch presenter."*
- **Smart Recommendations:** Suggests verified students from neighboring campuses (e.g., RVCE, PES, BMSCE, MSRIT) matching the exact missing skill.

---

## 2. "Squad Passport" (1-Click Team Registration Kit) 📋

### Problem Statement
Team leads spend 15–20 minutes per hackathon manually re-typing teammates' USNs, college emails, phone numbers, GitHub usernames, and LinkedIn URLs into external forms (Unstop, Devfolio, Google Forms).

### Proposed Solution
A 1-click **Export Registration Bundle**:
- Once a team roster is locked, the squad leader clicks **"Export Squad Passport"**.
- Instantly copies formatted text or generates a verified PDF team card with:
  - Team Name & Sanctioned Circuit ID (`#HK-XX`)
  - All 4 members' official college IDs, GitHub links, emails, and primary tracks
  - Ready to paste into any registration portal in seconds.

## 3. Karnataka Campus Circuit Leaderboard ("College Cup") 🏆

### Problem Statement
Collegiate engineering rivalries are fierce across Karnataka (e.g., RVCE vs. BMSCE vs. PES vs. NITK Surathkal), but hackathon wins remain isolated to personal LinkedIn posts without collective campus pride.

### Proposed Solution
A seasonal **Collegiate Tech Power Index**:
- Verified podium finishes (1st, 2nd, 3rd, Top 10) earn points for the students' respective colleges.
- A live district and college leaderboard ranking the top engineering institutions each semester.
- **Viral Growth Loop:** Colleges and tech clubs will actively promote BuildCrew to students to boost their campus standing on the state leaderboard.

---

## 4. Native 1-Tap Calendar Integration (`.ics` / Google Calendar) 📅

### Problem Statement
Students discover hackathons on the circuit but miss registration deadlines or presentation rounds weeks later because events aren't in their personal day-to-day calendars.

### Proposed Solution
- Add an **"Add to Google Calendar"** / **"Apple Calendar"** button on every hackathon card.
- Automatically creates calendar events with:
  - Exact registration deadline (with reminders at 24h and 2h before closing)
  - Sprint start and submission dates
  - Direct portal links and venue/Discord instructions.

---

## 5. WhatsApp & Telegram Squad Alert Bot 📲

### Problem Statement
Engineering students rarely monitor email inboxes, leading to missed squad invitations and expired registration windows.

### Proposed Solution
- A lightweight webhook bot integrated into the BuildCrew backend.
- Instant alerts for:
  - *"🚨 You received an invitation to join Team Nova for HackSprint 2026!"*
  - *"⏳ Registration for Devfolio Web3 Hackathon closes in 6 hours."*
  - *"🔍 Team CodeCrafters at RVCE is looking for 1 ML builder matching your profile."*

---

## 6. Hackathon Ideation & Pitch Deck Sandbox 💡

### Problem Statement
Teams frequently lose the first 8–12 hours of a 24-hour hackathon arguing about what idea to build and how to structure their presentation.

### Proposed Solution
A collaborative pre-sprint workspace inside the squad view:
- **Curated Problem Statements:** Real-world challenges categorized by tracks (Bangalore Urban Mobility, Karnataka Agritech, Micro-Fintech).
- **Pitch Deck Outline:** Standardized winning slide deck templates (Problem, Solution, Tech Stack, Business Viability, Demo).
- **Shared Team Canvas:** Interactive notes where members finalize tech choices before coding starts.

---

## 7. Play Store & Mobile Experience Optimizations 📱

### Proposed Solution
To ensure smooth Play Store deployment:
- **Offline Mode:** Local caching of bookmarked hackathons and squad rosters so students can check details even on spotty campus Wi-Fi.
- **Deep Linking:** Clicking a shared WhatsApp link (e.g., `buildcrew.com/hackathons/HK-04`) opens the native app directly to that specific event card.
- **Biometric Sign-in:** Fingerprint / Face Unlock for rapid access without password fatigue.
